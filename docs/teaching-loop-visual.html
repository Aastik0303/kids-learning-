Concept Loop — "A" padhana

Aapka loop, exactly: samjhao → poochho → suno → judge karo → decide karo. Galat hone par nayi strategy, wahi shabd nahi.

⚠ "Jab tak sahi na kare, move mat karo" — ek zaroori sudhaar

Aapka irada sahi hai: bachcha A seekhe bina aage na jaaye. Par agar rule ek sawaal pe lagaya jaaye, toh woh loop hamesha khatam hota hai — chahe bachche ne kuch na seekha ho. Neeche chart dekhiye: playgroup ke saare sawaal 2-option hain, toh 3 koshish mein 87.5% bachche sirf luck se "pass" ho jaayenge.

Aur ek 3-saal ka bachcha jo 3 baar fail ho chuka hai, jaanboojh kar random tap karne lagta hai — sirf loop se nikalne ke liye. Toh data bhi galat, aur seekhne ka anubhav bhi.

✓ Sudhaar: gate baithak pe nahi, concept pe

Baithak aage badhti hai. Curriculum nahi. A park ho jaata hai par "seekha hua" nahi banta — woh kal wapas aata hai, aur parso bhi, jab tak sach mein na aa jaaye. A pe depend karne wala har concept tab tak locked rehta hai (skill_graph.json).

Aapko jo chahiye tha woh mil gaya — bachcha bhi bach gaya.

03

9 strategies — "story ya example" ka poora set

Aapne kaha galat hone par story ya example do. Bilkul — par ek concept ko do tarah se samjhana kaafi nahi. Yeh poora set hai. Ek session mein ek strategy dobara nahi chalti.

S1 · TELL
Seedha bata do
Koi sawaal nahi. Sirf naam aur pehchan.
"Yeh A hai. A."
S2 · SHOW
Dikhao, batao mat
A chamakta hai, ungli uska shape banati hai, baaki dim.
↗ ↘ — beech mein patti
S3 · EXAMPLE
Bachche ki duniya se
Authored bank se — LLM invent nahi karta.
"A se Apple 🍎, A se Aam 🥭"
S4 · STORY
Chhoti kahani
≤3 vaakya. Har kahani ka ek feature — woh cheez jo woh sikha rahi hai.
"Ek Apple apna naam bhool gaya…"
S5 · CONTRAST
Sahi vs galat, saath
Discrimination sikhata hai — b/d, 6/9, circle/oval.
"Yeh A ✅ · Yeh B ❌"
S6 · DO-TOGETHER
Haath pakad ke
demo_tap — Pip tap karta hai, submit nahi karta. Phir bachcha.
Template-only, kabhi LLM-worded nahi
S7 · SENSE-SWITCH
Indriya badlo
Dekh ke nahi aaya? Ab chhuo, bolo, banao. Zero naya content chahiye.
tap ✕✕ → ab ungli se trace karo
S8 · PREREQUISITE
Peeche jao
Neev kamzor hai. Lesson pause, prereq micro-lesson, wapas wahi jagah.
A se pehle "khadi lakeer"
S9 · PARK
Aaj bas
Terminal action. Concept locked rehta hai, kal wapas aata hai.
"Kal pakdenge use 😊"

Playgroup (2–3 saal) mein S4 aur S5 legal hi nahi hain — us umar mein narrative comprehension aur fine discrimination abhi bane nahi hote. Yeh bands field se enforce hota hai, prompt ki guzarish se nahi.

6 hardness dials

H2 sabse under-rated hai. "Circle tap karo" with [circle, star] aasan hai. Wahi sawaal with [circle, oval] ek asli discrimination task hai. Ek hi authored activity, do bilkul alag difficulty — sirf distractor badalne se. Yeh muft range hai jo aaj use nahi ho rahi.

Step-down ka order ulta hai step-up se, aur woh ulta hona zaroori hai: jab bachcha atka hai pehle task aasan karo (H2 → H1), phir madad badhao (H6 → H3). Ulta karne se aap ek confusing task pe zyada madad de rahe hote ho — jo kaam nahi karta.

05

Reinforcement learning — haan, par kaunsa

Aapka loop ek asli MDP hai: state = bachche ki haalat is concept pe, action = 9 strategies, reward = seekha ya nahi, episode = ek concept pehle exposure se mastery tak. Yeh RL hai — bas isme LLM ke weights nahi badalte, teaching policy badalti hai.

STAGE 0 · DIN 1
Heuristic ladder
S1→S2→ASK→S3→S4→S5→S6→S7→PARK. Fixed order, deterministic. Yeh aapki baseline hai — iske bina aap kabhi jaan nahi paoge ki RL kaam kar raha hai.
Data chahiye: 0
STAGE 1 · ~2 HAFTE
Contextual bandit
Context coarse: (attempt × skill_type) = 16 buckets × 9 arms = 144 cells. Thompson sampling, nightly update, kill switch.
~4,300 events · pilot pe ~14 din · 6k DAU pe ~6 ghante
STAGE 2 · ~2 MAHINE
Context refinement
Jis bucket mein 200+ pulls ho gaye use split karo — age_band, phir mastery, phir fatigue. Sirf wahan split karo jahan data hai.
Poora state space kabhi pehle se nahi
STAGE 3 · ~4–6 MAHINE
Tabular Q-learning
864 states × 9 actions. γ=0.9. Ab delayed reward kaam karta hai — jo strategy agli strategy ko kaam karne deti hai, usko credit milta hai.
~78,000 events · Q warm-started from Stage 0
RLHF
Is list mein nahi
10,000+ human-ranked pairs, GPU training, apne weights serve karna — 12–18 mahine. Aur reward "bachche ne sahi jawab diya" ko maximise karne ka sabse tez raasta hai bachche ko jawab bata dena.
Stage 3 saturate hone ke baad socho — DPO, PPO nahi
✓ Do lines jo poori RL design ko bachati hain

−0.15 per extra attempt. Iske bina policy "efficiently padhana" nahi, "ragadna" seekhegi — kyunki agar sirf "aakhir mein sahi hua" ka reward hai, toh 8 koshish 2 koshish jitni hi achhi hain.

+0.80 agar kal yaad tha. Ek strategy jo aaj turant kaam karti hai par kal bhula di jaati hai, woh achhi teaching nahi hai. Isliye update nightly hai — yeh signal aane mein 24 ghante lagte hain.

Warm start optional nahi hai. Din 1 pe Q table random nahi, heuristic ladder hoti hai. Aap kabhi ek randomly-initialised policy bachchon pe nahi chalayenge. Worst case = baseline.
Exploration ε = 0.10, aur ε = 0 jab: attempt ≥ 3 · frustration ≥ 0.5 · pehla exposure · class = playgroup. Atka hua ya pareshaan bachcha experiment ki jagah nahi hai.

06

Jo kabhi agent ya RL ke paas nahi jaata

Jawab sahi hai ya galat
activity_evaluators.py — 37 registered types, pure functions.
Mastery ka number
EWMA + lazy decay. Arithmetic hai, judgement nahi.
Stars, unlock, certificate
Parent ko explain karna padta hai. "Model ka mood" jawab nahi hai.
Ownership & consent
Security aur kanoon. Kabhi inferred nahi.

Baaki sab kuch agent aur policy ke paas hai — plan, branch, strategy, example, scaffold, difficulty, UI, aur kab rukna hai. Beech mein ek Envelope hai: 26 deterministic rules jo veto kar sakti hain (attempt cap, answer-leak, repeat, shame, floors, budget, deadline). Har veto ek metric hai — aur veto rate hi aapka prompt-quality signal hai.
