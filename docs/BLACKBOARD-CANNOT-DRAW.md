# What the picture still cannot show

Generated from the syllabus against `app/playground/visuals.py`. A card gets
a picture when its **answer** names something the app can draw, and failing
that when the level's **title** does.

## What it can draw

- **Any letter, in any script**, written as a wipe - a glyph the app has
  never seen still appears.
- **Any number** up to three digits, and **counting** 1-10 as that many rings.
- **Eight shapes**: circle, square, triangle, rectangle, star, oval, diamond,
  semicircle.
- **Ten colours**, plus grey.
- **113 everyday things** - the animals, fruit, vehicles and
  objects `chalkArt.ts` holds hand-written strokes for. A person names these,
  never a model: `visuals.parse` refuses `thing:` outright.
